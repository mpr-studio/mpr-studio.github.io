name: Tom Dart
---

Tom Dart is the Sheriff of Cook County, Illinois, and runs the Cook County Jail, one of the largest single-site jails in the country. A former prosecutor and Illinois state legislator, he was first elected sheriff in 2006 and won his sixth term in November 2026. He has expanded job training inside the jail, including a culinary program, Recipe for Change, a two-acre farm and greenhouse, and construction training, alongside classes in art, chess, parenting and literacy. He has also become a national voice against the criminalization of mental illness, building treatment programs for people in and out of custody. In 2009, TIME named him one of its 100 most influential people. He lives on Chicago’s South Side and is the father of five, Molly among them.
