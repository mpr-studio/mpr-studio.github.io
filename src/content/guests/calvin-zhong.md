name: Calvin Zhong

Calvin Zhong (he/him) is a designer, educator and curator from New York’s Chinatowns. He was Curatorial Fellow in Architecture at MIT, where he taught in the MArch program and co-curated “Climate Work: Un/Worlding the Planet” at the Palazzo Diedo, Berggruen Arts & Culture, in Venice. He received his Master of Architecture, Master in City Planning and Bachelor of Science in Comparative Media Studies and Architecture at MIT. In 2023 he co-founded [AGONY](https://www.instagram.com/ago.nyc/), Architecture Group of New York, an architecture and urbanism practice.
