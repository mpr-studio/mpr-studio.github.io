Calvin Zhong is a designer and researcher. He studied comparative media studies and architecture at MIT (SB, 2018), and city planning and architecture there (MCP and MArch, 2024). He is a co-founder and partner of [AGONY](https://www.instagram.com/ago.nyc/), a design practice working on exhibitions, retail, graphics and digital design. At MIT he was design lead of the Local Code Lab's Drawing Together, which won the 2022 Norman B. Leventhal City Prize, and he worked with the Critical Broadcasting Lab on The Pilgrimage for the 2023 Venice Architecture Biennale and with the Future Heritage Lab on *Design to Live* (MIT Press, 2021).
