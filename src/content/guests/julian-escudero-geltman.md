Julian Escudero Geltman is a Brooklyn-based podcaster. He is also an architectural designer, critic and educator. He studied environmental studies at Oberlin College (2017) and architecture at MIT (MArch, 2023). Along with Ana Miljački, he co-produces [*I Would Prefer Not To*](https://archleague.org/project/i-would-prefer-not-to/), a podcast from MIT’s Critical Broadcasting Lab and the Architectural League of New York in which architects talk about the commissions they turned down, and which won the 2025 AIA Architecture in Media Award. He is a co-founding principal of [AGONY](https://www.instagram.com/ago.nyc/), Architecture Group of New York.
