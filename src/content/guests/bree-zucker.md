name: Bree Zucker
---

Bree Zucker is a former art dealer who now works in the beauty business, in what she calls corporate America. In 2020, when the pandemic had closed New York’s museums and galleries, she curated *Titan* with the artist Damián Ortega and the gallery kurimanzutto: an exhibition in the phone booths of Sixth Avenue, free and open at all hours, with a performance by Yvonne Rainer on the public plaza of a bank and a donation to a local food charity.
