Matt Miller and Molly Dart read their vignettes from the precedents report: Matt’s “On the Jail Lineage,” on how New York has kept its jails out of sight, from the Tombs to Rikers to the tower now going up on the Tombs site, and “On Prison Abolition”; and Molly’s “The Threshold of the Jail,” on how visitation shapes where a jail meets the city, and “The Cost of Community – Compensation?,” on the city grant that bought 215 Centre Street. With guest Tom Dart, Sheriff of Cook County, who runs the Cook County Jail, they and Rodrigo Escandón Cesarman discuss Rikers and how distance cuts people in jail off from their families, tall jails as a threat on the skyline, the visitation center Dart is building for children, abolition and improving lives now, private prisons, how people inside listen to the radio, and how Chinatown might take to the new jail.
